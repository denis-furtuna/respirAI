Checks

    Record a test clip

    10 seconds, kept on this phone only. Listen to it, or save it as a WAV: 8 kHz, 16-bit, the same format the AI gets.

    What to try
    1. Tap the chest piece or the table next to the mic: the level should jump to −20 dBFS or more.
    2. Seal test: note the background with the chest piece lying on the table, then hold it firmly on your chest and stay still. A good airtight seal on the tube gives a clearly lower background.
    3. Breathe deeply with the chest piece on your chest: the waveform swells with each breath. Record a clip and listen.
    4. Range: walk away with the phone. The lost packets show where the connection gets weak.
