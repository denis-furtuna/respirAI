Our AI needs more lungs. Lend it yours.

We are students who built a digital stethoscope around a €5 pharmacy one. We lend it to you for free, for a limited time. You breathe for two minutes a day. It learns.

How it works

  1. You leave your email. That is the whole form. Joining commits you to nothing.
  2. We contact you when a device is free. The first batch is small, so not everyone gets one at once.
  3. You borrow it for a fixed period. We tell you the exact dates, and what we ask of you, before you accept.
  4. You do a short check each day. The app shows you where to hold the chest piece, and a voice guide talks you through it.
  5. You send it back. The next person on the list gets it.

What it is, honestly

respirAI is an early prototype made for a hackathon. Its AI learned from 126 patients recorded with other stethoscopes, and on our own device it is not reliable yet. That is why we need recordings from it.

It is not a medical device and it does not diagnose. Do not use its results to make health decisions. If you are unwell, see a doctor. In an emergency, call 112.

For you, at home

  • The device is free to borrow.
  • You need an Android phone with Chrome. It does not work on iPhone yet.
  • About two minutes a day.

For family doctors

  • A free pilot with a few devices for your patients.
  • Recordings between visits, with each patient's written consent.
  • You tell us what would make it useful in practice.

Your data

What do you keep from the waitlist form?

Your email, and whether you are joining for yourself or as a clinician. Nothing else. We use it to contact you about the waitlist, and we delete it if you ask.

Do my lung recordings leave my phone?

Not by default. The app runs its AI on your phone and keeps recordings there (privacy policy). Sharing recordings with us to train the AI is a separate, explicit choice, which we ask for in writing before the loan starts. You can say no.

If I agree to share, what happens to the recordings?

We store them without your name, use them only to train and test respirAI, and delete them if you ask.
