What it is, and what it is not.

Your recordings stay with you

The AI runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose, in writing, to share recordings to help it learn. Read the privacy policy.

Honestly, an early prototype

respirAI was built at a student hackathon. Its AI learned from 126 patients recorded with other stethoscopes, and on our own device it is not reliable yet. That is why we need recordings from it.

It does not diagnose. If you are unwell, see a doctor. In an emergency, call 112.

Questions

Anything else? Ask us on GitHub.

Is respirAI a medical device?

No. It is a student prototype for learning and research. It does not diagnose, and its results should not be used to make health decisions.

Can I buy one?

Not yet. For now we lend prototypes for free, for a limited time, while the AI learns to hear through our device.

Which phones does it work with?

Android phones, in Chrome. The app talks to the device over Bluetooth, which browsers on iPhone do not support, and there is no iPhone app yet.

Do my lung recordings leave my phone?

Not by default. The app runs its AI on your phone and keeps recordings there. Sharing recordings with us to train the AI is a separate, explicit choice, which we ask for in writing before the loan starts. You can say no. If you agree, we store them without your name, use them only to train and test respirAI, and delete them if you ask.

What do you keep from the waitlist form?

Your email, and whether you are joining for yourself or as a clinician. Nothing else. We use it only to contact you about the waitlist, and delete it whenever you ask.

Can I try the app without the device?

Yes. Open the app and use its practice device to walk through a full check.
